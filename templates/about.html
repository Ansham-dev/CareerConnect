About CareerConnect

CareerConnect is an online job portal that bridges the gap between job seekers and employers.

For Job Seekers

Register, build a profile, upload your resume, search and filter jobs by location, category and salary, apply online and track your application status from a personal dashboard.

For Employers

Create a company profile, post job vacancies, edit or remove postings, review applicants, download resumes and shortlist or reject candidates.

For Administrators

Admins verify employers before they can post, manage user accounts and job categories, and remove fraudulent or inappropriate postings to keep the platform trustworthy.
